- Bender et al. (2021), Brown et al. (2020), Ouyang et al. (2022), and
  Hewing & Leinhos (2024) are the academic/research papers, used to back
  specific claims (bias risks, what AI models can do, the named prompting
  techniques).
- Apple, Google, CleverTap, and Reteno are industry sources — used only to
  check the character-limit figures quoted in Prompts 3 and 9 against the
